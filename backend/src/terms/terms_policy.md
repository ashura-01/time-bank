# TimeBankV2 Official Terms and Policy

## What is TimeBankV2?

TimeBankV2 is a community-driven skill and time exchange platform. Our core
philosophy is that everyone's time is equal: one hour of service provided
equals one hour of time credit earned, regardless of the service type. There
is no money involved — only time.

## How does the Time Balance and Escrow system work?

When a user books an **Offer** (someone volunteering a skill), the cost in
hours is immediately deducted from the requester's available time balance
and placed into a secure escrow (held balance). This prevents overdrafts and
guarantees the provider will be paid once the job is confirmed complete.

## Who pays whom for a Request-type service?

This works differently from an Offer, and it's a common point of confusion:
for a **Request** (someone asking the community for help), it is the person
who **fulfills** the request — the provider — whose hours are held in escrow
and deducted. The person who **posted** the request — the requester —
receives the time credit once the service is completed. In short: whoever
does the work gets paid, regardless of whether the listing was an Offer or
a Request; only whose balance is checked and held differs.

Users cannot book or request a service if their available time balance is
insufficient to cover the hours involved.

## What's the difference between an Offer and a Request?

- **Offer:** A user volunteers a skill they possess (e.g. "I will mow your
  lawn"). Requesting an Offer holds funds from the requester.
- **Request:** A user asks the community for help with a task (e.g. "I need
  someone to fix my sink"). Fulfilling a Request holds funds from the
  fulfiller (the provider), not the original poster.

Services can be conducted either in person at a specified location, or
remotely.

## What are the stages of a transaction?

- **Pending** — requested, not yet accepted.
- **Confirmed** — the provider has agreed to perform the service.
- **Completed** — both parties have independently confirmed the service was
  rendered, and time credits have been transferred. TimeBankV2 requires
  confirmation from both sides before any credits move — one person marking
  it done is not enough on its own.
- **Cancelled** — aborted before completion; any escrowed funds are
  returned to whoever they were held from.
- **Disputed** — a disagreement has been raised; the transaction is frozen
  pending administrator review.

## How do I raise a dispute, and what happens next?

If a service is unsatisfactory, or a user fails to show up, either party
can raise a dispute. Once raised, the transaction's status becomes
"disputed" and is frozen. A TimeBankV2 administrator reviews the submitted
evidence and makes a final decision.

Currently, when an administrator resolves a dispute, the held funds are
released and the transaction is marked completed — the provider is paid.
If instead the transaction is cancelled rather than resolved, any escrowed
funds are returned to whoever they were held from.

## Can I get my money back if something goes wrong?

Escrowed hours are only ever released two ways: through a normal completed
transaction (both parties confirm), or through a cancellation, which
returns the held funds to whoever they were taken from. Raise a dispute if
you believe a transaction should not proceed as agreed — an administrator
will review it.

## What are the community guidelines?

After a transaction completes, both users are encouraged to leave a 1–5
star rating and a written review. Users must treat each other with
respect. Hate speech, harassment, or repeatedly failing to honor confirmed
transactions will result in an immediate and permanent ban from
TimeBankV2.
